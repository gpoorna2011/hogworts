Ravenclaw

The House of Wit and Learning
Founder:

Rowena Ravenclaw

Head of House

Professor Filius Flitwick.
  • Professor of Charms (teaching essential spells like Wingardium Leviosa) at Hogwarts
  • Duelling Champion: In his youth, he was a "Master Duellist," making him incredibly dangerous in combat despite his gentle nature.
  • Goblin Ancestry: Flitwick is part-goblin (a "dash" of goblin blood), which accounts for his short height and likely contributes to his formidable magical talent.
  • Courageous: Don't let the squeaky voice fool you; he fought bravely in the Battle of Hogwarts, defeating the high-ranking Death Eater Antonin Dolohov.

    • What is it like to be an Ravenclaw

    Symbol: The Eagle (representing the ability to soar to heights others cannot reach).

    Ravenclaws are defined by their sharp minds and curiosity.

    Core values

  • Wisdom: The ability to apply knowledge in meaningful ways.
  • Wit: A quick, clever, and often humorous intelligence.
  • Intellectual Curiosity: A drive to understand the "why" behind everything.
  • Individualistic: You are comfortable in your own skin and aren't afraid to be called "eccentric."
  • Visionary: You might sometimes be described as "away with the fairies," lost in your own creative thoughts.
  • The Innovators: Most of the wizarding world’s greatest inventors and pioneers were Ravenclaws.

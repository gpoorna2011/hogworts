Head of House

Professor Pomona Sprout.
  • Professor of Herbology (the study of magical plants and fungi).at Hogwarts
  • She treats her plants and her students with great care and patience.
  • She led the defense of the castle by using dangerous magical plants (like Mandrakes and Venomous Tentacula) as weapons against the invading Death Eaters.
  • While she is generally kind and easygoing, she is incredibly brave and fiercely protective of her house and the school.

    Core values

  • Hard Work: You believe that nothing beats a job well done through honest effort.
  • Loyalty: You are the person people can always count on, through thick and thin.
  • Resourcefulness: The ability to find ways to get things done, no matter the obstacles.
  • Fairness: You have a strong moral compass and believe in equality for everyone.
  • Patience: You understand that great things take time and persistence
  • Trustworthy: You are known as the "best friend" because your word is your bond.

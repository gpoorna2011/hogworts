    • What is it like to be an Slytherin

    Symbol: The Serpent (representing resourcefulness and sleekness).

    Slytherins are known for their calculating minds and long-term goals.

    Core values

  • Ambition: A strong desire to reach the top and achieve big things.
  • Shrewdness: A sharp, clever mind that knows how to navigate complex situations.
  • Resourcefulness: The ability to find ways to get things done, no matter the obstacles.
  • Strategic: You are always "one step ahead" and usually have a Plan B.
  • Polished: You take significant pride in your appearance and how the world perceives you.
  • Guarded: You prefer to keep your "soft side" hidden and maintain a cool, composed exterior.

    • Famous Alumni

    Slytherin has produced some of the most powerful and influential magical figures in history:

  • Merlin: The most famous wizard in history (a fact often forgotten by those who judge the house).


  • Severus Snape: A master of Potions and a complex hero.


  • The Malfoy Family: Known for their influence and status within wizarding society.
